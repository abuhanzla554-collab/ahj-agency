Common questions

Pricing, explained

Why don't you list fixed prices?

Every project differs in scope, competition, number of pages or campaigns and required support. A quote keeps the deliverables clear.

Is advertising budget included?

Unless a proposal specifically says otherwise, platform ad spend is separate from management fees.

Can you promise a first-page Google ranking?

No responsible SEO provider can guarantee a specific ranking. We can work on technical quality, relevance, content and local search foundations.
